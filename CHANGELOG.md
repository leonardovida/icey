# Changelog

Full notes for every version are in [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases), the main place for Icey updates. A copy of each release's notes is kept in [release-notes/](release-notes/). Changes that need you to do something are listed in [BREAKING_CHANGES.md](BREAKING_CHANGES.md).

## [0.13.0](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.13.0)

Makes Icey lighter on CPU, energy and the window server: closed windows stop redrawing, idle timers pause, drags elsewhere no longer reach Icey, and the appearance setting shrinks from about 63 KB to under 1 KB. Installed copies of Icey 0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1 and later update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.13.0.md).

## [0.12.0](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.12.0)

The first version with Icey's own number. It brings together every Icey release so far, with a full guide to the features and fixes since Ice. Installed copies of Icey 0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1 and later update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.12.0.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.6](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.6)

Translates Icey into nine languages, adds a Displays pane with settings for each display and an Ice Bar option by display width, updates Sparkle to 2.10, and stops item spacing from force quitting apps. Installed copies of earlier Icey 0.11.13 versions update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.6.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.5)

Fixes fullscreen detection on every macOS version, a launch crash from bad settings values and a Spacing hang, and adds an optional Ice Bar fade, with fixes ported from open Ice pull requests. Installed copies of earlier Icey 0.11.13 versions update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.5.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.4](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.4)

Stops Mission Control from triggering Icey, keeps items opened from the Ice Bar from staying visible, ends Ice Bar hangs on macOS 26, and fixes or improves more Ice bugs. Installed copies of earlier Icey 0.11.13 versions update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.4.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.3](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.3)

Adds the Ice Bar per display, a choice of where new items appear, settings export and import, and a Dock option, and fixes or improves about 30 more Ice bugs. Installed copies of earlier Icey 0.11.13 versions update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.3.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.2](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.2)

A Liquid Glass app icon and search panel on macOS 26, and 12 more Ice bugs fixed. Installed copies of 0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1 update automatically. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.2.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1)

Adds macOS 26 Tahoe and early macOS 27 support, carries the performance work over to the new code, and fixes about 40 upstream Ice bugs. Menu bar appearance settings reset once during the upgrade. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1.md).

## [0.11.12-icey.1](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.12-icey.1)

First Icey release. It is based on Ice 0.11.12 and cuts CPU, energy and WindowServer usage. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.12-icey.1.md).

# Changelog

Full notes for every version are in [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases), the main place for Icey updates. A copy of each release's notes is kept in [release-notes/](release-notes/). Changes that need you to do something are listed in [BREAKING_CHANGES.md](BREAKING_CHANGES.md).

## [0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1)

Adds macOS 26 Tahoe and early macOS 27 support, carries the performance work over to the new code, and fixes about 40 upstream Ice bugs. Menu bar appearance settings reset once during the upgrade. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1.md).

## [0.11.12-icey.1](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/releases/tag/v0.11.12-icey.1)

First Icey release. It is based on Ice 0.11.12 and cuts CPU, energy and WindowServer usage. See the [release notes](release-notes/v0.11.12-icey.1.md).

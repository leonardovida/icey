## Upgrading from 0.11.12-icey.1 or Ice

Install this version by hand once. Earlier versions check Ice's update feed, so they won't offer it.

1. Quit Ice or Icey.
2. Download `Icey-0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1.dmg` below, open it and drag `Icey.app` to Applications.
3. Move the old `Ice.app` to the Trash.
4. Open Icey. Coming from Ice, grant Accessibility and Screen Recording again. Coming from Icey 0.11.12-icey.1, they carry over.
5. Set your menu bar appearance again (tint, shadow, border, shape). It resets once with this update.

From now on, Icey updates itself from its own releases. Turn on "Automatically check for updates" in Settings > About.

## Features

- **macOS 27:** hiding, the Ice Bar, search and the layout editor work. Settings that do nothing on macOS 27 are hidden ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).
- **New in Ice 0.11.13, now in Icey:** a Liquid Glass design, a choice of section divider style, and an option to turn off the menu bar's right-click menu ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).
- **Updates come from Icey**, signed with Icey's key. Ice's updater can no longer replace Icey ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).

## Bug Fixes

- **Crashes:** clicking the menu bar or the Icey icon, or turning on the Ice Bar with the Dynamic location, could crash the app. It doesn't anymore.
- **Update prompt:** "Check for updates automatically?" could hide behind windows, ignore clicks and come back on every launch. It now works and stays answered.
- **Empty layout on Tahoe:** the Menu Bar Layout pane showed "Unable to display menu bar items". It now shows your items.
- **Hung apps:** an app that stopped responding could freeze Icey and leave the menu bar hidden. Icey now gives up after 1 second.
- **Ice Bar:** it could close before the pointer reached it, or leave an empty bar on screen after a fullscreen change. Both are fixed.
- **Multiple displays:** fullscreen and the split shape now behave per display.
- **Performance:** the CPU and energy savings from 0.11.12-icey.1 carry over to the new code.

Every fixed Ice issue, with what was wrong and what changed, is on the [Ice bugs fixed in Icey](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/) page.

## Compatibility

- **The app is now `Icey.app`.** Settings carry over. Remove the old `Ice.app`, and if you use "Launch at login", turn it off and on again.
- **Menu bar appearance resets once.** Set it again in Settings > Menu Bar Appearance.
- **"Show section dividers" is now "Section divider style".** Your choice is migrated.
- **macOS 27 support is new.** Please [report problems](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/issues/new/choose).
- **Permission still asked after granting it?** Remove the app from the list in System Settings with the minus button, then add it again.

Everything that needs action is also in [BREAKING_CHANGES.md](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/blob/main/BREAKING_CHANGES.md).

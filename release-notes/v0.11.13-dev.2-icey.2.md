## Highlights

- **A Liquid Glass look on macOS 26.** A new layered app icon, a glass search panel and icons in Icey's menu ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12)).
- **12 more Ice bugs fixed**, including items from apps with several icons, such as OneDrive, and menu bars that hide automatically. See the [full list](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/) ([#11](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/11), [#14](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/14)).

## Features

- **App icon:** a layered icon that macOS 26 renders with Liquid Glass in the default, dark, clear and tinted styles. Before, macOS 26 showed the old icon on a gray backing ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12)).
- **Search panel:** a glass panel on macOS 26, like Spotlight. On every version, the field has a search icon, and a search with no matches says so ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12)).
- **Menu:** on macOS 26, each item in Icey's menu has an icon ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12)).
- **Permissions window:** clearer text, a checkmark for granted permissions, and Return to continue ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12)).

## Bug Fixes

- **Several items from one app:** clicking one OneDrive item could hide the others or show the wrong one. Each item is now tracked on its own ([jordanbaird/Ice#857](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/857), #739, #404).
- **Displays without a menu bar:** with "Displays have separate Spaces" off, clicks and right-clicks at the top of a second display could trigger Icey. They now don't ([jordanbaird/Ice#456](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/456), #646, #639, #383).
- **Menu bar that hides automatically:** show on hover, click and scroll, and the Ice Bar, only worked with the pointer against the screen edge. They now work across the whole menu bar ([jordanbaird/Ice#708](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/708), #286, #331).
- **Mouse wheels:** show on scroll only worked with a trackpad. It now works with a wheel too ([jordanbaird/Ice#717](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/717)).
- **Hide application menus:** it only worked with the always-hidden section turned on. It now works without it ([jordanbaird/Ice#434](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/434)).
- **Items stuck in view:** an item opened from the Ice Bar could stay in the menu bar when its neighbor had changed. It now goes back to the section it came from ([jordanbaird/Ice#729](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/729)).

## Compatibility

- **macOS 14 and 15:** the app icon is a flat version of the new icon, generated from it.

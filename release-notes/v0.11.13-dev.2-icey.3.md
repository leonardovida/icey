## Highlights

- **Four of the most requested Ice features.** Use the Ice Bar only on some displays, choose where new menu bar items appear, export and import your settings, and keep Icey out of the Dock while Settings is open. Each is off by default, so nothing changes until you turn it on ([#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).
- **About 30 more Ice bugs fixed or improved:**
  - Items no longer drift into the wrong section across restarts.
  - Rehide no longer closes the menu or popover you're using.
  - "Hide application menus" works.
  - Option-click works with Caps Lock on.
  - The menu bar styling comes back after login and sleep.

  See the [full list](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/) ([#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17), [#20](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/20)).

## Features

- **Ice Bar on chosen displays:** Settings > General > Use Ice Bar > **Use on**. Choose All displays, Displays with a notch, or Built-in display. On other displays, hidden items show in the menu bar as if the Ice Bar were off. Useful with a MacBook and a wide external display ([jordanbaird/Ice#223](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/223), [#703](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/703), [#797](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/797), [#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).
- **Where new menu bar items appear:** Settings > General > **New items appear in**. Choose Visible, Hidden or Always-Hidden. When an app you haven't used with Icey adds an item, Icey moves it there once and then leaves it alone, so items you rearrange stay where you put them. Not available on macOS 27 ([jordanbaird/Ice#767](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/767), [#6](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/6), [#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).
- **Export and import settings:** Settings > Advanced > **Export Settings…** saves Icey's settings to a file. **Import Settings…** loads one, for example on another Mac, and relaunches Icey. The order of menu bar items and item spacing aren't included, because macOS keeps those ([jordanbaird/Ice#326](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/326), [#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).
- **No Dock icon while Settings is open:** turn off Settings > Advanced > **Show Icey in the Dock while Settings is open** to keep Icey out of the Dock and the app switcher ([jordanbaird/Ice#724](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/724), [#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).
- **Star Icey on GitHub:** the About pane has a button to star the project, in place of the old donate button ([#18](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/18)).

## Bug Fixes

Each fix says what went wrong before, and what happens now.

- **Items moving to the wrong section after a restart:**
  - Before: macOS saves where a menu bar item sits only when that item is dragged, and Icey's dividers never are. After a restart the dividers came back at old positions, so visible items or the Icey icon landed in the hidden section, and hidden items in the always-hidden section. Repairing the divider order also pushed every hidden item into always-hidden at once.
  - Now: Icey saves where its icon and dividers are when it quits, and repairs the order without emptying the hidden section ([jordanbaird/Ice#769](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/769), [#702](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/702), [#732](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/732)).
- **Rehide closing the menu or popover you're using:**
  - Before: clicking a switch or dragging a slider in another app's popover, or using a hidden item's menu, hid the items and closed it.
  - Now: rehide waits while a menu or popover is open below the menu bar, and clicks inside one don't count as clicks outside ([jordanbaird/Ice#403](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/403), [#526](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/526), [#621](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/621), [#452](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/452)).
- **"Hide application menus":**
  - Before: macOS could refuse Icey's request to become the active app, so only a Dock icon appeared and the menus stayed. Showing the always-hidden section right after the hidden one measured the items before they moved. After a restart, the Dock icon could stay for minutes.
  - Now: Icey takes focus when its request is refused (unless you switched apps), waits for the items to move, and gives the menus back as soon as no section is shown ([jordanbaird/Ice#879](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/879), [#620](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/620), [#144](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/144), [#808](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/808)).
- **Caps Lock and the Fn key:**
  - Before: with Caps Lock on, Option-click and Control-click acted like plain clicks, and items clicked in the Ice Bar or search never opened.
  - Now: only Shift, Control, Option and Command count ([jordanbaird/Ice#644](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/644), [#845](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/845)).
- **Menu bar styling missing after login or sleep:**
  - Before: if the menu bar wasn't there yet when Icey started, or after the displays changed during sleep, the tint, border or shape didn't appear until a space change or a restart of Icey.
  - Now: Icey keeps trying for about two minutes, and again whenever the displays wake ([jordanbaird/Ice#409](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/409), [#53](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/53)).
- **Menu bar shape size:**
  - Before: the inset shape and the Ice Bar cut off items on a notched display at a lower resolution. The full shape was a few pixels short on displays without a notch. The split shape's right half could hold a long blank stretch.
  - Now: the inset never makes the shape shorter than an item, square-ended shapes fill the menu bar's height, and the right half ends at the last item ([jordanbaird/Ice#260](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/260), [#657](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/657), [#608](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/608), [#676](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/676)).
- **Menu Bar Layout pane:**
  - Before: after you dragged an item to another section, the section it came from stopped updating until the pane was reopened. A failed move left the item drawn where you dropped it. Items touching a divider at a fractional position on a scaled display were missing.
  - Now: every section stays up to date, a failed move redraws the item where it really is, and those items show ([jordanbaird/Ice#283](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/283), [#300](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/300), [#233](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/233), [#861](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/861), [#918](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/918), [#655](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/655)). Partly fixed.
- **An item shown from the Ice Bar staying visible, partly fixed:**
  - Before: if the item's app was quitting or relaunching when it was due to go back, Icey forgot it, and it stayed visible from then on.
  - Now: Icey keeps trying for about a minute and a half ([jordanbaird/Ice#513](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/513)).
- **Pointer disappearing, partly fixed:**
  - Before: Icey hid the pointer while putting shown items back, including while it waited for you to stop moving the mouse.
  - Now: it's hidden only for the moment each item moves ([jordanbaird/Ice#751](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/751), [#757](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/757)).
- **Clicks triggering Icey by mistake, partly fixed:**
  - Before: moving between an app's menus, or clicking the top of a fullscreen app on a second display, could show the hidden items.
  - Now: neither does ([jordanbaird/Ice#567](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/567), [#899](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/899)).
- **Smaller fixes:**
  - The item spacing slider shows your saved value after launch ([jordanbaird/Ice#392](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/392)).
  - The Icey icon reappears properly when you turn it back on ([jordanbaird/Ice#329](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/329)).
  - The permissions window links straight to the right System Settings list ([jordanbaird/Ice#1004](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/1004)).

These fixes come from reading the code and the reports. Most weren't reproduced on the affected setups, such as multiple displays, ultrawide displays or the specific apps named in the reports. Please [report](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/issues/new/choose) anything that still happens.

## Compatibility

- **New settings keep today's behavior.** Nothing changes until you pick something else in the new settings.
- **Very wide displays:** on displays wider than about 5,000 points, some hidden items can still show. macOS limits how far Icey can push them ([jordanbaird/Ice#94](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/94)).

## Highlights

- **Mission Control no longer sets Icey off.** Hidden items don't appear while you use Mission Control, and the menu bar styling isn't drawn on top of it ([#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24)).
- **Fewer stuck and stray items.** Items opened from the Ice Bar go back where they belong. Moving an item no longer clicks it by accident in apps like Mullvad VPN and ProtonVPN ([#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24)).
- **No more Ice Bar hangs on macOS 26.** The Ice Bar opens within a second even when macOS is slow to report the menu bar items ([#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24)).

## Bug Fixes

Each fix says what went wrong before, and what happens now.

- **Mission Control:**
  - Before: moving the pointer to the top of the screen in Mission Control showed the hidden items or opened the Ice Bar. After a space was deleted or switched in Mission Control, the menu bar styling was drawn over the space switcher.
  - Now: Icey recognizes Mission Control and waits for it to close ([jordanbaird/Ice#714](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/714), [#216](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/216)).
- **Wallpaper from the previous space:**
  - Before: after switching spaces, the split or rounded shape kept showing the previous space's wallpaper.
  - Now: Icey uses the wallpaper of the space on screen, and captures it again once the switch has settled ([jordanbaird/Ice#336](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/336), [#337](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/337)).
- **Flicker when toggling the Dock:**
  - Before: showing or hiding the Dock with Option-Command-D made the menu bar styling flicker.
  - Now: the styling is rebuilt only when a display really changes ([jordanbaird/Ice#435](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/435)).
- **Apps opening by themselves, partly fixed:**
  - Before: when Icey hid an item again, it sent an extra mouse release that some apps, such as Mullvad VPN and ProtonVPN, took for a click, so their window opened.
  - Now: Icey sends the extra release only if the item didn't respond to the first one ([jordanbaird/Ice#766](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/766), [#586](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/586)).
- **Items from the Ice Bar staying visible, partly fixed:**
  - Before: an item clicked in the Ice Bar could stay in the visible section, for example when a Live Activity next to it changed size, or when its app re-added its item on click.
  - Now: Icey checks that the item really went back to its section and moves it there if not, and no longer mistakes a re-added item for an open menu ([jordanbaird/Ice#729](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/729), [#513](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/513), [#799](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/799)).
- **Ice Bar and Icey hanging on macOS 26, partly fixed:**
  - Before: if reading the menu bar items stalled, the Ice Bar never opened again, and Icey could stop responding with an external display connected.
  - Now: the Ice Bar opens within a second either way, and Icey's item helper can no longer tie up the rest of the app ([jordanbaird/Ice#888](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/888), [#790](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/790), [#865](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/865)).
- **Wrong item clicked, partly fixed:**
  - Before: clicking an item in the Ice Bar could open the Apple menu instead.
  - Now: Icey never clicks at a point that's off every display. It shows the item first ([jordanbaird/Ice#642](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/642)).
- **Clicks in fullscreen with the Icey icon off, partly fixed:**
  - Before: with the Icey icon turned off, clicking the top of a fullscreen app showed the hidden items.
  - Now: Icey checks whether the menu bar is really showing ([jordanbaird/Ice#899](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/899)).
- **Menu bar appearance doing nothing, partly fixed:**
  - Before: without the Screen Recording permission, tint, border, shadow and shape settings had no effect, with no explanation.
  - Now: the appearance editor says the permission is missing and links to it, and the appearance applies as soon as it's granted ([jordanbaird/Ice#627](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/627), [#525](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/525)).
- **Items that can't be moved:**
  - Before: moving an item such as an iPhone Live Activity failed with "took too long to respond".
  - Now: the alert says the item didn't move and that some items may not allow moving ([jordanbaird/Ice#704](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/704)).

These fixes come from reading the code and the reports. Most weren't reproduced on the affected setups. Please [report](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/issues/new/choose) anything that still happens.

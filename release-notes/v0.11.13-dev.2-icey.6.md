## Highlights

- **Icey speaks nine more languages.** German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, picked from your Mac's language settings ([#32](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/32)).
- **Settings for each display.** A new Displays pane lets each display use the Ice Bar or not, and show its hidden items automatically. The Ice Bar can also switch on by display width ([#33](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/33)).
- **Safer updates and spacing.** Icey's updater moves to Sparkle 2.10 with its security fixes, and applying item spacing no longer force quits your apps ([#29](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/29), [#30](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/30)).

## Features

- **Languages:** Icey follows System Settings > General > Language & Region. To use a different language for Icey only, add it under Applications there. Most translations come from community pull requests to Ice by gmkrxb ([jordanbaird/Ice#1002](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/1002)) and keithadler ([jordanbaird/Ice#983](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/983)). The rest were machine-assisted, so corrections are welcome ([#32](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/32)).
- **Displays pane:** Settings > **Displays** lists your displays ([#33](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/33)). For each one:
  - **Use Ice Bar:** Follow General setting, On or Off.
  - **Show hidden items automatically:** hidden items stay shown in the menu bar while you work on that display. If you hide them yourself, they stay hidden until you move to another display.
  - Displays you disconnect keep their settings, and you can forget them.
  - Based on [jordanbaird/Ice#612](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/612) by Amir Pourmand and [#413](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/413) by Michele Primavera and Lucas Garron.
- **Ice Bar by display width:** Settings > General > Use Ice Bar > Use on > **Displays narrower than…**, with a width you choose. Wider displays show hidden items in the menu bar. Based on [jordanbaird/Ice#795](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/795) by Roni Laukkarinen ([#33](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/33)).

## Bug Fixes

- **Applying item spacing closed apps without asking:**
  - Before: Apply force quit any app that didn't quit within a second, so an app asking to save a document lost it.
  - Now: apps get ten seconds to quit. An app that doesn't quit, for example because it asks to save, stays open and is listed so you can restart it yourself. Only macOS's own menu bar helpers, which hold no documents, are still restarted by force ([#30](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/30)).

## Compatibility

- **New settings keep today's behavior.** The Displays pane and the width option change nothing until you use them.

## Maintenance

- **Sparkle 2.10.0:** the update framework moves from 2.8.0, picking up its security fixes for installing updates ([#29](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/29)).
- **Cleanup:** a check for menu bars that hide automatically never ran, so it's removed. Nothing changes for you ([#31](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/31)).
- **Ice issue list:** 166 more Ice issues are on the [fixes page](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/), so every Ice issue that Ice left open or closed without a fix is now listed there ([#34](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/34)).

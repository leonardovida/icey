## Highlights

- **Icey 0.12 is the first version with Icey's own number.** Earlier Icey versions carried Ice's last version, 0.11.13-dev.2, with "-icey" added.
  - 0.12.0 brings together everything from the seven Icey releases so far.
  - If you're coming from Ice, this page is the full picture of what changed.
- **A menu bar manager that works on today's macOS.** Icey runs on macOS 14 Sonoma, 15 Sequoia, 26 Tahoe and 27, and uses far less CPU, energy and WindowServer memory than Ice.
  - More than 300 issues reported against Ice are fixed or improved.
- **Features Ice users asked for:**
  - settings for each display
  - an Ice Bar that only appears where you want it
  - a choice of where new items land
  - export and import of your settings
  - Icey in nine more languages

## Upgrading from 0.11.13-dev.2-icey.6

Nothing to do. The version number changes, but your settings, item layout, hotkeys and permissions stay as they are.

1. Wait for Icey to offer the update, or choose Settings > About > **Check for Updates**.
2. Install it, and Icey restarts on its own.

The update reaches every Icey version from 0.11.13-dev.2-icey.1 on, because updates are ordered by build number, not by version name.

### Coming from Ice

Icey replaces Ice and keeps its settings, because it uses the same bundle identifier (`com.jordanbaird.Ice`).

1. Quit Ice.
2. Download `Icey-0.12.0.dmg` below, open it, and drag `Icey.app` to Applications.
3. Move the old `Ice.app` to the Trash, because two apps with the same identifier confuse macOS.
4. Open Icey and grant Accessibility, and optionally Screen Recording. macOS asks again because Icey is signed by a different developer.
5. If you used "Launch at login", turn it off and on again in Settings > General.
6. If your menu bar appearance was reset, which happens once when coming from Ice 0.11.12, set the tint, shadow, border and shape again.

See [Compatibility](#compatibility) for the details, and [BREAKING_CHANGES.md](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/blob/main/BREAKING_CHANGES.md) for everything that ever needed action.

## Features

### Works on macOS 26 Tahoe and macOS 27

- **macOS 26 Tahoe:** the layout settings, the Ice Bar and search all work ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).
  - Ice 0.11.12 showed "Unable to display menu bar items" in all three on Tahoe.
  - Ice 0.11.13-dev.2 improved this, but still crashed when you clicked its icon.
- **macOS 27:** hiding items, the Ice Bar, search and the layout editor work. Ice doesn't run there at all.
  - Settings that have no effect on macOS 27 are hidden: the divider style, "Hide app menus when showing menu bar items" and the temporary show interval. "New items appear in" isn't available there yet.
  - Items without a bundle identifier can't be moved between sections, and disabled controls say why ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).

### Settings for each display

Settings > **Displays** lists every connected display with its name, size, and whether it's built in or has a notch ([#33](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/33)). For each display you can set:

- **Use Ice Bar:** Follow General setting (the default), On, or Off. On and Off override the General settings for that display only.
  - For example, keep the Ice Bar on your MacBook's notched screen and turn it off on a wide external monitor.
- **Show hidden items automatically:** while you work on that display, your hidden items stay shown in the menu bar, as if you had clicked the Icey icon. Useful on a large monitor with plenty of room.
  - If you hide them yourself, they stay hidden until you move to another display.
  - Automatic rehide leaves them alone.
  - The always-hidden section still hides as usual.

macOS shows the same menu bar items on every display, so with two displays Icey follows the display you're working on and switches when you move. Displays you disconnect keep their settings under "Not Connected", where you can forget them. This feature is based on community work for Ice by Amir Pourmand ([jordanbaird/Ice#612](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/612)), and Michele Primavera and Lucas Garron ([jordanbaird/Ice#413](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/413)).

### Choose where the Ice Bar appears

The Ice Bar shows hidden items in a bar below the menu bar, which helps on screens where a notch or a crowded menu bar hides them. Settings > General > Use Ice Bar > **Use on** now offers ([#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19), [#33](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/33)):

- **All displays:** the Ice Bar everywhere, as in Ice.
- **Displays with a notch:** only on screens with a camera notch.
- **Built-in display:** only on your Mac's own screen.
- **Displays narrower than…:** only on displays narrower than a width you choose, 1800 points by default. The Displays pane shows each display's width.
  - Based on [jordanbaird/Ice#795](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/795) by Roni Laukkarinen.

On the other displays, hidden items show in the menu bar as if the Ice Bar were off. The Displays pane can override this for a single display.

- **Fade in and out:** turn on Settings > General > Use Ice Bar > **Fade in and out** for a short fade when the Ice Bar opens and closes. It's off by default.
  - Adapted from [jordanbaird/Ice#1002](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/1002) by gmkrxb ([#27](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/27)).

### Choose where new menu bar items appear

In Ice, a newly installed app's item could land anywhere, often in the always-hidden section. Settings > General > **New items appear in** lets you choose Visible, Hidden or Always-Hidden ([#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).

- **How it works:** when an app you haven't used with Icey adds an item, Icey moves it there once. After that it leaves the item alone, so items you rearrange stay where you put them.
- **Limit:** some apps remove and recreate their item, for example on every launch or for every call. macOS places the new item at the far left, so it can still end up in a hidden section.
  - Move it back in Menu Bar Layout.
- Not available on macOS 27.

### Export and import your settings

Settings > Advanced > **Export Settings…** saves Icey's settings to a file. **Import Settings…** loads one, for example on a new Mac or after reinstalling, and restarts Icey ([#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).

- **What's included:** the General, Hotkeys, Advanced, Displays and appearance settings.
- **What isn't:** the order of your menu bar items and the item spacing, because macOS keeps those, and "Launch at login".
- Values that don't make sense, such as a hotkey that doesn't exist, are skipped instead of crashing Icey ([#27](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/27)).

### Icey in nine more languages

Icey is now in German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, in addition to English ([#32](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/32)).

- **Choosing a language:** Icey follows System Settings > General > Language & Region. To use a different language for Icey only, add Icey under Applications there.
- **Where the translations come from:** most come from community pull requests to Ice by gmkrxb ([jordanbaird/Ice#1002](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/1002)) and keithadler ([jordanbaird/Ice#983](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/983)). The rest were machine-assisted.
- **Corrections welcome:** if a word reads oddly, a pull request to `Ice/Localizable.xcstrings` is the quickest fix.

### Smaller additions

- **Keep Icey out of the Dock:** turn off Settings > Advanced > **Show Icey in the Dock while Settings is open** to keep Icey out of the Dock and the app switcher ([#19](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/19)).
- **Liquid Glass on macOS 26** ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12)):
  - a layered app icon that follows the default, dark, clear and tinted styles
  - a glass search panel, like Spotlight, that says when nothing matches
  - icons next to the items in Icey's menu
- **From Ice 0.11.13:**
  - Settings > Advanced > **Section divider style**.
  - An option to turn off the right-click menu in the menu bar, Settings > Advanced > **Enable secondary context menu**, for when it clashes with other apps ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).
- **Clearer permissions:** the permissions window explains each permission, shows a checkmark once it's granted, and links straight to the right list in System Settings. The appearance editor says when Screen Recording is missing, since the menu bar styling needs it ([#12](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/12), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17), [#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24)).
- **Updates from Icey:**
  - Icey updates itself from its own releases, signed with its own key, so Ice's updater can no longer replace it.
  - The update framework is Sparkle 2.10, with its latest security fixes for installing updates ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5), [#29](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/29)).
- **Star Icey on GitHub:** the About pane has a button to star the project ([#18](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/18)).

## Bug Fixes

Icey has gone through every issue reported against Ice. 219 are fixed and 90 more are improved. The [Ice bugs fixed in Icey](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/) page lists each one, with what went wrong and what Icey does now. These are the ones you're most likely to notice.

### Speed, energy and memory

- **Energy use while moving the mouse:** Ice listened to every mouse move, drag and scroll in the system, even with show on hover off. Icey listens only while a setting needs it ([jordanbaird/Ice#334](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/334), [#1](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/1)).
- **CPU spikes after switching apps:** with menu bar styling on, Ice polled every millisecond after each app switch. Icey backs off, and only polls for the split shape ([jordanbaird/Ice#578](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/578)).
- **WindowServer memory growing:** once the Ice Bar had been shown, Ice captured the menu bar every 5 seconds forever, and its image cache never shrank. Icey captures only while the Ice Bar is on screen and prunes its cache ([jordanbaird/Ice#819](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/819), [#530](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/530)).

### Crashes and freezes

- **Crashes on macOS 26:** clicking the Icey icon or the menu bar, or turning on the Ice Bar, crashed Ice 0.11.13-dev.2. Fixed ([jordanbaird/Ice#669](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/669), [#786](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/786), [#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).
- **Freezes caused by other apps:** an app that stopped responding could freeze Ice for seconds at a time and leave the menu bar hidden. Icey gives up after one second ([#1](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/1)).
- **The Ice Bar not opening on macOS 26.** Partly fixed. ([jordanbaird/Ice#888](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/888), [#790](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/790), [#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24))
  - Before: if macOS was slow to report the menu bar items, the Ice Bar never opened again, and Icey could stop responding with an external display connected.
  - Now: the Ice Bar opens within a second either way.
- **A crash at every launch:** an out-of-range hotkey value, for example from an imported file, crashed Ice each time it started. Icey ignores such values ([#27](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/27)).

### Items in the right place

- **Items drifting between sections.** ([jordanbaird/Ice#769](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/769), [#702](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/702), [#946](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/946), [#20](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/20))
  - Before: after a restart, visible items, or the Icey icon itself, could turn up in a hidden section, and hidden items in always-hidden. macOS remembers where an item sits only after it's dragged, and Icey's dividers never are.
  - Now: Icey saves where its dividers are when it quits, and repairs their order without emptying the hidden section.
- **Items opened from the Ice Bar staying visible.** Partly fixed. ([jordanbaird/Ice#513](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/513), [#729](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/729), [#799](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/799), [#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24))
  - Before: an item you clicked in the Ice Bar or search sometimes never went back. This happened when its app re-added it, when a Live Activity next to it changed size, or when its app was restarting.
  - Now: Icey checks that the item really returned to its section, and keeps trying for a while.
- **Apps with several items, such as OneDrive with two accounts:** clicking one hidden item made Ice treat all of them as shown, and it could put the wrong one back. Icey tracks each item on its own ([jordanbaird/Ice#857](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/857), [#11](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/11)).
- **The Menu Bar Layout pane going stale.** Partly fixed. ([jordanbaird/Ice#283](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/283), [#918](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/918), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17))
  - Before: after a drag, the section you dragged from stopped updating, and a failed move left the item drawn where you dropped it.
  - Now: both redraw.

### Showing and hiding

- **Menus and popovers closing under you:** with smart rehide, clicking a switch or dragging a slider in another app's popover hid the items and closed it. Icey waits while a menu or popover is open ([jordanbaird/Ice#403](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/403), [#621](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/621), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17)).
- **Mission Control:** reaching for a space at the top of the screen showed your hidden items or opened the Ice Bar. Icey recognizes Mission Control and waits for it to close ([jordanbaird/Ice#714](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/714), [#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24)).
- **Fullscreen apps.** Partly fixed. ([jordanbaird/Ice#899](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/899), [#27](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/27))
  - Before: Icey misread which spaces are fullscreen, so the styling and show on click and hover could act up there. Clicking the top of a fullscreen app could also show your items.
  - Now: Icey reads the space type correctly and only reacts while the menu bar is showing.
- **Caps Lock:** with Caps Lock on, Option-click and Control-click acted like plain clicks, and items clicked in the Ice Bar never opened ([jordanbaird/Ice#644](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/644), [#845](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/845), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17)).
- **Second displays:** with "Displays have separate Spaces" off, Ice swallowed clicks at the top of every display, such as on browser tabs. Icey reacts only where there is a menu bar ([jordanbaird/Ice#456](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/456), [#11](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/11)).
- **A menu bar that hides itself:** show on hover, click and scroll only worked with the pointer pressed against the screen edge. Now the whole menu bar counts once it slides in ([jordanbaird/Ice#708](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/708), [#11](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/11)).
- **Mouse wheels:** show on scroll only worked on a trackpad. One notch of a wheel now works ([jordanbaird/Ice#717](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/717), [#11](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/11)).
- **Apps opening by themselves.** Partly fixed. ([jordanbaird/Ice#766](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/766), [#586](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/586), [#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24))
  - Before: hiding an item again sent it an extra mouse release, which apps like Mullvad VPN and ProtonVPN took as a click, so their window opened.
  - Now: Icey no longer sends that extra release.
- **"Hide app menus when showing menu bar items":** macOS often refused to let Ice take over the menu bar, so only a Dock icon appeared and the menus stayed. Icey takes focus properly and gives the menus back as soon as nothing is shown ([jordanbaird/Ice#879](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/879), [#144](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/144), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17)).
- **A shortcut that blocked typing:** a shortcut saved for the always-hidden section stayed active while that section was off. Option-A, for example, stopped typing "å" everywhere ([jordanbaird/Ice#498](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/498), [#27](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/27)).

### Menu bar styling

- **Styling missing after login or sleep:** if the menu bar wasn't ready when Icey started, or the displays changed during sleep, the tint, border and shape didn't appear. Icey keeps trying, and tries again when the displays wake ([jordanbaird/Ice#409](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/409), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17)).
- **Shapes on the wrong display.** Partly fixed. ([jordanbaird/Ice#910](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/910), [#445](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/445), [#6](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/6))
  - Before: pieces of the shape could float in the middle of another display, or cover a fullscreen app on it.
  - Now: each display has its own styling and fullscreen state, and styling that lands on the wrong display hides itself.
- **Wallpaper from the previous space:** the split and rounded shapes showed the wallpaper of the space you had left. Icey uses the current one ([jordanbaird/Ice#336](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/336), [#337](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/337), [#24](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/24)).
- **Shape sizes:** the inset shape cut items off on notched displays, and the full shape fell a few pixels short elsewhere. Both now fit ([jordanbaird/Ice#260](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/260), [#17](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/17)).

### Item spacing and updates

- **Applying item spacing:** Ice force quit any app that didn't quit within a second, so an app asking to save a document lost it. An app that survived could also leave Apply waiting forever.
  - Icey gives each app ten seconds and never force quits it. Apps that stay open are listed, so you can restart them yourself.
  - Only macOS's own menu bar helpers, which hold no documents, are still restarted by force ([#27](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/27), [#30](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/30)).
- **The spacing slider:** it showed the default instead of your saved value after launch ([jordanbaird/Ice#392](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/392)).
- **The update prompt:** "Check for updates automatically?" could hide behind windows, ignore clicks and come back on every launch. It works now ([#5](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/5)).

Many of these fixes come from reading the code and the reports, and couldn't be reproduced on every setup, especially with several displays. If something still happens, please [report it](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/issues/new/choose).

## Compatibility

- **Version numbers:** Icey now counts its own versions from 0.12.0. Nothing else changes: the app is still `Icey.app`, the bundle identifier is still `com.jordanbaird.Ice`, and updates keep coming from Icey's releases.
- **Requirements:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel.
- **New settings keep the old behavior.** Everything above that's optional is off until you turn it on: the Displays pane, the Ice Bar choices, new item placement, the Ice Bar fade, and the Dock option.
- **Screen Recording is optional.** Without it, the Menu Bar Layout pane and the menu bar styling don't work, and the appearance editor says so. Hiding and showing items still work.
- **Very wide displays:** on displays wider than about 5,000 points, some hidden items can still show. macOS limits how far Icey can push them ([jordanbaird/Ice#94](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/94)).

## Maintenance

- **Builds:** every release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- **Fixes from Ice pull requests:** Icey includes fixes from community pull requests that Ice never merged, with their authors credited in the commits. They are listed at the end of the [fixes list](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/blob/main/docs/upstream-issues.md#upstream-pull-requests-included-in-icey).
- **Release scripts:** the release scripts and RELEASING.md use the new version numbers in their examples.

## Highlights

- **Fewer crashes and freezes.** Icey no longer reads the list of running apps off the main thread, an event tap can no longer be used after it is freed, and show on hover no longer freezes Icey while the frontmost app is not responding ([#59](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/59), [#60](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/60), [#62](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/62)).
- **Fix a permission that stopped applying, in one click.** After moving from Ice or after an update, the permissions window can reset the old entry so macOS asks again ([#58](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/58)).
- **Items stay where you put them.** A shown item you drag stays there, and quitting Icey puts shown items back in their section ([#57](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/57), [#61](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/61)).

## Features

- **Reset and Ask Again:** when Accessibility or Screen Recording shows as enabled in System Settings but Icey still asks for it, this button in the permissions window removes Icey's old entry and asks again. If the reset fails, the window says so, and you can still remove the entry in System Settings ([#58](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/58), [jordanbaird/Ice#1004](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/1004)).

## Bug Fixes

- **Item moved back after you dragged it:** an item shown from the Ice Bar or search and then Command-dragged somewhere else went back to its old place. Now it stays where you put it ([#57](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/57), [jordanbaird/Ice#1024](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/1024)).
- **Shown items left visible after quitting:** quitting Icey while an item was shown left it in the visible section from then on. Icey now moves it back before quitting ([#61](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/61), [jordanbaird/Ice#513](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/513)).
- **Zoom jumping around:** with Zoom on, the zoomed area jumped whenever Icey moved an item in the background. Icey now waits until you zoom out ([#63](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/63), [jordanbaird/Ice#486](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/486)).
- **Missing appearance on a display:** when a display's menu bar appearance landed on another display, Icey hid it and left that display without a tint until the next space change. It now tries again on the right display ([#55](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/55), [jordanbaird/Ice#1023](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/1023)).
- **Freeze with show on hover:** with an app that was not responding in front, Icey could freeze while the pointer was over the menu bar. Show on hover now checks the app's menus at most once a second and skips apps that are not responding ([#62](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/62)).
- **Possible crashes:** Icey reads the running and frontmost apps on the main thread, the same kind of problem as the crash on quit fixed in 0.14.0 ([#59](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/59)). An event tap can no longer be used after it is freed ([#60](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/60)). On macOS 27, a menu bar item image that cannot be copied is skipped instead of crashing Icey ([#56](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/56)).
- **Permissions window:** clicking Grant Permission twice could leave Icey waiting forever for the permission. Now every request finishes once the permission is granted ([#58](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/58)).

More fixed Ice issues are listed on the [fixes page](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/).

## Compatibility

- **Quitting with a shown item:** quitting takes up to 3 seconds longer while an item is shown, so it can go back to its section first. Nothing to do.
- **Zoom:** while the screen is zoomed in, a shown item stays in view and new items keep their place until you zoom out. Nothing to do.

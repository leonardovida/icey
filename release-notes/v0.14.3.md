## Highlights

- **Control Icey from Shortcuts, scripts and launchers.** `icey://` links show and hide sections, open search, toggle the Ice Bar and more, so Raycast, Alfred, Shortcuts and Terminal can drive Icey ([#74](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/74)).
- **Less flicker and less window server work with a menu bar shape.** The wallpaper behind the shape is captured when it changes, or once a minute, instead of every 5 seconds ([#77](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/77)).
- **Quicker access to the layout.** Right-click an empty part of the menu bar and choose Edit Menu Bar Layout ([#73](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/73)).

## Features

- **`icey://` links:** open them from Shortcuts (Open URLs), Terminal (`open icey://toggle-hidden`), Raycast, Alfred or any app ([#74](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/74), [jordanbaird/Ice#382](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/382)).
  - `toggle-hidden`, `show-hidden`, `hide-hidden`, and the same for `always-hidden`
  - `search`, `toggle-ice-bar`, `toggle-application-menus`, `toggle-auto-rehide` and `settings`
  - The README lists every link under Automation.
- **Edit Menu Bar Layout in the right-click menu:** right-clicking an empty part of the menu bar opens Menu Bar Layout directly ([#73](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/73), [jordanbaird/Ice#497](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/497)).
- **Hotkey to pause automatic rehiding:** Settings > Hotkeys > Toggle automatic rehiding turns Automatically rehide on or off ([#76](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/76), [jordanbaird/Ice#18](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/18)).
- **Longer timed rehide:** with the Timed strategy, items can stay shown for up to five minutes, instead of 30 seconds ([#75](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/75), [jordanbaird/Ice#39](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/39)).
- **Control-N and Control-P in search:** they select the next and previous item, like the arrow keys ([#72](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/72), [jordanbaird/Ice#641](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/641)).
- **Remove Color Stop:** right-click a stop in the gradient tint to remove it ([#78](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/78), [jordanbaird/Ice#369](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/369)).

## Bug Fixes

- **Video flickering every 5 seconds:** with the full or split shape, Icey captured the wallpaper every 5 seconds, and protected video, such as in the TV app, flickered each time. Icey now captures it only when the wallpaper changes, or once a minute for dynamic wallpapers. Setting Shape to None stops the captures entirely ([#77](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/77), [jordanbaird/Ice#105](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/105)).
- **Huge Ice Bar or Menu Bar Layout:** another app's hiding item, such as BetterTouchTool's, is thousands of points wide and stretched the Ice Bar across the screen or made Menu Bar Layout scroll. Items are now drawn at most 300 points wide there ([#79](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/79), [#81](https://github.com/leonardovida/icey/pull/81), [jordanbaird/Ice#379](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/issues/379)).

More fixed Ice issues are listed on the [fixes page](https://leonardovida.github.io/icey/fixes/), which now covers 734 Ice issues.
